### Project Description
COIN'T FLIP is a deliberately over-engineered coin-flip experience that turns a simple decision into a dramatic 3D animation. It adds a little chaos, comedy, and spectacle to everyday indecision, making even the smallest choice feel like a cinematic event.

### The Problem (that doesn't exist)
People have been using a simple coin toss to make decisions for years, but no one has yet made that experience visually dramatic enough to rival a blockbuster trailer. Most coin flips are boring, predictable, and not emotionally satisfying.

### The Solution
(that nobody asked for)
We built a fun and exaggerated digital coin toss with a 3D animated motion, gravity-inspired physics, playful outcomes, and an intentionally chaotic design. The result is a decision-maker that is equal parts useful, ridiculous, and memorable.
